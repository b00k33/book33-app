How many steps a day?

Each flight is 10 steps, drawn as a row of 10. You go up once and down once, so every step is counted twice a day.

On the calendar

The plan puts one small chip in each day's all-day row — you don't place anything. Tap it and the day's stairs open: one tick per place. On Wed and Sat (your workout rest days) the chip still shows, because you still climb the stairs.
