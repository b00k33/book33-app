How it behaves

Opens by itself once, the first time the Fitness page loads after this build, and never again on its own. After that the dashed Sort 2 left › chip at the end of the type row opens it, and it disappears when every exercise has a type.

One tap per exercise. The tapped symbol fills, its word appears under the name, and the bar above moves (5 of 7 → 6 of 7). Tap the same symbol again to take it off.

Nothing is guessed. No exercise has a type until you pick it. A type belongs to the exercise name, so it follows that exercise into every plan, log and My exercises entry.

Each row’s sub-line is the plan it is in and its sets, so you can tell two similar names apart.

Picking a type starts a fresh row sensibly — and never touches a row you tuned

Only while the row is untouched (still its first sets, no hand-set rest, Timer or Left & right). A small note says what it did, with Undo.

What is stored, and the rules it keeps

Types

Per exercise name, beside timed / sided / rest / area / gear (EX_MODE_PREFS), so a type follows an exercise into every plan, planned day and log. The list of types is book33-fitness-types: her five (stab, flex, str, iso, ply) plus any “+ New type” you add. Hiding a type keeps every exercise that has it.

Colour stays = muscle group. A type is a drawn symbol and a word, never a new hue.

Goals

book33-fitness-goals = [{id, name, kind: row | hold | weight | tick, n, reps?, by?, done?, doneAt?}]. One active goal per exercise name. Progress is computed from FIT_LOG on every draw, never stored.

In a row = best single set (Left & right: the lower of the two sides). Hold = longest hold. Weight = heaviest set with at least the goal’s reps. I can do it = you tick it.

What stays as it is

The weekly Gym / F45 / Bouldering goal (fitWeekTarget) is not a goal kind and is not duplicated. Timer, Left & right and rest keep their own controls; a type only sets them on a fresh row.

Nothing is ever deleted by this: a type can be hidden, a goal can be reached or removed with Undo.
