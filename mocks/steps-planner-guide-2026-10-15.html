What I tidy while I am in here
  1. “Place” becomes “Name” — one box that fits a walk or a staircase.
  2. Each row shows only its own boxes. A walk has no “flights”; stairs have no “steps one way”. Nothing greyed out.
  3. Days: one tap for Mon–Fri. “Weekdays” and “Every day” chips set them in one tap (the day letters still work for single days).
  4. The chip and the sheet say “Steps” instead of “Stairs”, with a footprints icon (the stairs icon stays on the stairs rows).

On the calendar

Still one automatic chip per day in the all-day row. Tap it and the day opens as a list: one tick for each leg (so there and back are two ticks), one for each staircase. The chip fills as you tick. Try it: tap the ticks, or “+ Extra steps”.
