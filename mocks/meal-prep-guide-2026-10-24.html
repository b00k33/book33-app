The Breakfast event, tapped on the calendar

This replaces the single "Log what I ate →" row of a meal event. Same block on Lunch and Dinner.

Show
  1. The meal and where it came from: "From every Breakfast" or "Just today".
  2. Prep ahead lists the recipe's steps. Tap to tick; the count updates.
  3. Ingredients read the fridge. Missing ones show "on Shopping List" and are already on it.
  4. Had it logs the planned meal at the time you tap. "Log something else →" opens the usual sheet.

Change: just this day, or every Breakfast

Tap Change. The joined bar says how far the swap goes. Your recipes come first, then your own dish, then eating out.

  1. Every Breakfast sets the default. Every day without its own pick follows it, today included.
  2. Just this day changes only this date. The popup then shows "Just today" and a "↺ Every Breakfast" chip to go back.
  3. With no default yet, the bar starts on Every Breakfast; once one exists it starts on Just this day.
  4. An own dish or eating out has no prep or ingredients. It still gets Had it.

Food page · This week's meals

Breakfast joins the grid. The "Every day" row is the same default the event uses. A day's own pick is solid, a day following the default is paler. Everything missing goes to the Shopping List, as today.

On the calendar

The meal events keep their usual look. The planned meal reads under the title, and a ✓ shows once you tap Had it. Lunch has nothing planned yet, so it stays as it is today.

  1. Tap a meal block to open the event from section 2.
  2. Prep shows as 0/2 on the block until it is done.
  3. Eating out reads "Out · Café near work".

Your picks so far

  • A default, and you change a day. The whole repeating Breakfast gets one default.
