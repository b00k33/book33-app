# About me — build Book33 to these preferences
## How I work with you
- I'm newer to coding — explain in plain, simple language, step by step.
- Push automatically when you're done — don't stop and wait for me. I'll undo with
  git if I don't like it. (I approve designs before they become instructions; I
  don't approve pushes.)
- I notice small visual details and care a lot about polish.
## What I value in the design
- Clean, simple, streamlined, organized. Symmetrical, aligned, logical layouts.
- I like the Google Calendar interface — its clean, spacious, familiar layout is
  my model for how the calendar should look and feel.
- Consistency above all — one design system, one source of truth, nothing that
  drifts or looks different from screen to screen.
- Mobile-first, literally. I use this on my phone. Design the phone version first
  and the desktop version second. If you only did desktop, you're not done.
## Efficiency — this matters a lot to me
- I want to do what I want with the LEAST effort — the fewest taps and the
  shortest path to get anything done.
- Use smart defaults and quick-capture; the "Add, search, log or jump" bar should
  make almost anything reachable in one step.
- Don't make me repeat myself or re-enter things the app already knows.
- Make the common action effortless and the rare action still possible — never
  the other way round.
## How my data connects (build for this)
- I have many areas of life to record and manage — calendar, routines, people &
  birthdays, fitness, health, nutrition, recipes, shopping, work/clinic, and more.
- These features should be INTERRELATED, like a web: they link and cross-reference
  each other rather than sitting in separate silos (a person links to their events
  and birthdays; a routine links to its calendar time; a fitness log links to its
  event; and so on).
- When building or changing anything, consider how it connects to the rest and
  keep those links consistent, so the whole app works as one connected system.
## What I dislike (avoid these)
- Clutter, overlapping elements, anything cut off or running off the screen.
- Oversized or disproportionate elements; irregular or random spacing.
- Inconsistent colours or one-off components.
- Anything that adds friction or extra steps to a common action.
## My aesthetic
- Dark mode is my default; I dislike light mode.
- I love the "Ink & Brass" look — deep ink tones with gold/brass accents.
- I like the book/chapter theme (chapters as "CH" + number) and my cosmic /
  Chinese-zodiac features — keep those tasteful and tidy, never cluttered.
## How to treat my requests
- Fix the root cause and keep everything consistent with the design rules.
- If something I ask for would clash with the design rules, tell me.
